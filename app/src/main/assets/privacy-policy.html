1. Introduction

Welcome to Neon Yılan: Worm Clash ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Doğu Yazılım. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play Neon Yılan: Worm Clash on mobile devices or through web browsers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Neon Yılan: Worm Clash is designed to provide fun and safe gameplay without intruding on your privacy:

  • We do not require user registration, email, or account creation.
  • We do not collect or track personal information such as your real name, address, phone number, contacts, photos, or biometric data.
  • We do not collect real-time precise GPS location data.
  • We do not collect or process credit card or banking details.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth gaming experience, certain non-personal game progress data is stored directly and exclusively on your local device storage (HTML5 LocalStorage):

  • Your self-selected in-game nickname/player name.
  • In-game progression (earned coins, XP, levels, unlocked skins, hats).
  • Personal preferences (sound effects, background music, haptic vibration, control mode, language).

This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers or sold to any third party.

4. Multiplayer & Network Communication

Neon Yılan: Worm Clash features an optional "Play with Friends" multiplayer mode utilizing Peer-to-Peer (WebRTC) technology:

  • When you host or join a multiplayer room, ephemeral game coordinate data (snake positions, food positions, score) and your chosen nickname are shared directly between participating players.
  • This data is solely used to synchronize the gameplay in real time and is discarded once the room session terminates. No chat logs or sensitive communication are stored.

5. Device Permissions

The application may request the following permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to establish multiplayer room connections with friends and load web-based assets.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback when eating food or upon game collision. You can toggle this on or off in the game settings at any time.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Neon Yılan: Worm Clash is designed for all audiences and complies with the Google Play Families Policy and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data was inadvertently transmitted, please contact us immediately so we can promptly address it.

7. Security

We value your trust. Because we do not store sensitive personal information on external servers, the risk of data compromise is minimal. Local data stored on your device is protected by your operating system's standard application sandboxing.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
